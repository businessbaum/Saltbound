# The second boat: a 14 m gaff cutter with a top deck and a lower deck (design proposal, D51)

Owner (2026-10-04): "start designing a new boat with a top deck and under deck and with more space."

![Plan sheet](Boat2_Plans.png)

The sheet is generated by `Tools/design/draw_boat2_plans.py` (profile with the rig, deck plan, lower deck, midship
section with a 1.5 m figure). It is a proposal for review: nothing is built in the game yet.

## Concept

A working boat a sailor could live on: bigger than the first boat in every direction, still sailed by one person.
A gaff cutter (one mast, gaff mainsail, staysail and jib on a bowsprit) keeps the rig and the sailing model of the
first boat (D41: set sail = go, take in = slow down), only bigger. Same hand-crafted style (D38): planked hull, hewn
spars, weathered paint (oxblood with a cream sheer stripe), chunky fittings.

## Top deck

- **Foredeck:** windlass and cathead (anchor), a cargo hatch over the hold, the bowsprit.
- **Amidships:** the mast with its rope points (set / take in the sails at the shrouds, as now), a deckhouse
  (0.75 m high, 2.4 m wide) with a skylight and portholes; the companionway at its aft end leads below.
- **Aft:** a raised quarterdeck (0.55 m) with the wheel (the wheel saved from the first boat, D45) and the mainsheet.
- Boarding: gaps in the bulwark midships with ladders on both sides (D43), a gangway when moored.

## Lower deck

Sole (floor) 0.75 m below the waterline: 1.65 m clear under the deck beams, 2.45 m under the deckhouse (the player is
1.5 m tall).

- **Hold** (forward): cargo and the boat's own stores (a chest for the satchel's overflow: items have a home).
- **Saloon** (midships, under the deckhouse and skylight): table, benches that double as bunks, stove, chart table
  (opens the map, D50), lanterns.
- **Captain's cabin** (aft, under the quarterdeck): bunk (sleep / save point), sea chest.
- Ladders: the companionway ladder from the deckhouse, a ladder up through the cargo hatch.

## Technical plan and risks

1. **Hull shape per boat:** `SaltHullShape.h` is the first boat's constants. The second boat needs its own shape
   (the same formulas with other numbers, plus a transom): a hull shape struct per boat class, used by the physics,
   the collision capsules and the ocean's hull cut-out.
2. **The sea inside the hull** (D42) must follow each boat's shape: the cut-out reads the active boat's shape numbers
   from `MPC_Ocean` instead of constants.
3. **Below the waterline inside the hull**, three systems must know the player is in the boat, not in the sea:
   - the underwater look (the post-process tests the camera against the wave surface): skip it inside the hull volume;
   - swimming (the movement checks the player's immersion): no water inside the hull;
   - the wake and the trail stay outside (they already are: the cut-out removes them inside).
4. **Walking below deck on a moving boat:** the interior floors, bulkheads, ladders and deckhouse need collision for
   the player (UCX boxes, as the deck now); the boat's own contacts with the world stay capsules. Ladders below use
   the stepped climb (D43).
5. **Light below deck:** dark by default (Lumen); the skylight, portholes and lanterns light it. A test map shot
   checks it is readable, not black.
6. **Physics:** about 30 t; the same model (buoyancy columns, foils, sail drive) with its numbers scaled; the boat
   suite gets a copy for the second boat.

## Steps

1. **Owner review** of this sheet (questions below).
2. **Blockout** in Blender and in the game: hull, decks, deckhouse, ladders, simple interior volumes; walk it, check
   headroom, light and the sea inside (items 1-4 above).
3. **Owner review** of the blockout in the editor.
4. **Hand-crafted art pass** (planking, rig, weathering, interior props) and the second boat's tests.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Rig:** a cutter (one mast, proposed) or a ketch (a second, smaller mast aft: more sail, more to handle)?
2. **Stern:** a transom with a raised quarterdeck (proposed: more room aft) or a double-ender like the first boat?
3. **Helm:** the wheel on the quarterdeck (proposed), or a tiller?
4. **Size:** 14 m (proposed), or smaller (12 m, more nimble) / bigger (16 m, more rooms)?
5. **How the player gets it:** an upgrade of the first boat, found (a wreck to repair), or built in the harbour?
