# Saltbound: the first hour (M6)

The first playable hour, beat by beat: what the player does, what exists today and what is still to build. It turns the concept's
first forty minutes (`GameConcept.md` 8.1) into a plan for the world as it is now (D60-D74). Recommendations are marked; the open
choices are at the end.

### What holds it together
- **Soft gates, no walls:** the boat cannot sail until the sail is mended; the sail needs the cache's sailcloth; the cache needs the
  shovel and the log's sketch. Everything else is open: a player who wanders first still finds the chain.
- **The clock does the pacing:** the morning is for learning (the beach, the Keeper, the cache, the lagoon); dusk calls the player
  out with the Spire's light; the night makes the way home tense; the Hollowkeel's lights are the relief.
- **A fast player** reaches the Spire by day: the tower is a ruin with a closed panel; the light (and the rubbing) wait for the
  night, and the log says so ("the light on the Spire burns again"). **A slow player** sees the light while still in the lagoon: the
  call comes earlier.
- **If the player never sails out:** the next morning the Keeper says "Still here? The light was burning again last night." Nothing
  breaks.
- **Saving:** at the pier, aboard the Hollowkeel and on sleeping; a voyage always ends somewhere warm.

## 5. The owner's choices (2026-10-06, D77)

1. **Where the player wakes:** on the south-west beach by the wreck at dawn.
2. **The sail at the start:** ripped; the cache's sailcloth is needed to sail.
3. **The Spire's light:** from the first dusk, every night; the rubbing can only be taken at night.
4. **People in v1:** simple stylised figures behind their counters.

A castaway owns nothing: the Keeper gives the shovel and a lantern, the Cartographer's sea chest their compass and spyglass (and the
log, FH3). Play-in-editor and the tests still start on the pier with the tools; the prologue runs on "New voyage" and with
`sb.Story.Prologue`.
